Claude and ChatGPT each have a directory of connectors. A member who finds you there connects with a click or two instead of pasting an address. A listing is yours: you submit it under your company's name and the assistant's maker reviews it.

You don't need a listing to start. Members can [add your address by hand](/mcp/connect) today. A listing is worth it once you have many members or want the reach.

## Before you apply

Both directories ask for roughly the same things:

- Your library on [your own domain](/mcp/own-domain), with [member sign-in](/mcp/member-sign-in) if your content is paid
- A privacy policy and terms of service on your website
- A support contact
- An icon and a short description
- A test login the reviewers can use, with access to some content

## Claude

Submit at [claude.ai/directory/manage](https://claude.ai/directory/manage) from a paid Claude plan. Anthropic's [submission guide](https://claude.com/docs/connectors/building/submission.md) lists the current requirements. Enter your MCP address as the connector's URL.

## ChatGPT

OpenAI lists connectors as apps. Its [submission guide](https://developers.openai.com/apps-sdk/deploy/submission) asks for a verified organization, proof that you own the MCP domain, test cases and a short video walkthrough. A listing has one fixed address, which is another reason to move to your own domain first.

## What we do

Tell us before you submit. We answer the reviewers' technical questions, and we can check your setup against the current requirements. Neither company states how long a review takes.
