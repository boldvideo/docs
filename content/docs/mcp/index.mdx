Your members already work in Claude, ChatGPT and similar tools. Bold gives your library an address they add there once. From then on they ask their assistant a question and the answer comes from your videos, with links to the exact moment.

It runs on the [Model Context Protocol](https://modelcontextprotocol.io) (MCP), an open standard, so any tool that supports MCP can connect.

<Callout title="Availability">
  This is switched on per account. If you don't see **Claude & ChatGPT** under **Settings**, [ask us](mailto:help@boldvideo.com) to turn it on.
</Callout>

## What members get

The assistant gets one tool, `bold_ask`. It answers a question from your library's transcripts and returns the sources: the video, the timestamp and a link. It is the same engine as the [AI chat](/guides/ai-features) in your portal.

The assistant cannot browse, download or change anything.

## Turn it on

1. In Bold, open **Settings** and choose **Claude & ChatGPT**.
2. Turn the switch on.
3. Give it a **name** (what members see when they add it, at most 30 characters) and a **description**. The description tells the assistant what your library covers and when to use it, so write it for the assistant: who the host is, which topics, what kind of questions.
4. Copy the **MCP address**. This is what members add in their assistant.

Turning it off stops every connected assistant on its next question. Nobody has to connect again when you turn it back on.

## Who can use it

| Mode | Who gets answers | From which videos |
|------|------------------|-------------------|
| **Anyone with the address** | Anyone who has the address | Public videos only |
| **Members sign in** | Members who sign in on your site | The collections your site says that member may use |

A new library starts open. If your content is paid, set up [member sign-in](/mcp/member-sign-in) before you share the address.

## What you see

- **Settings › Claude & ChatGPT** shows how many members are connected and how many questions came in over the last 30 days.
- **People** lists the connected members.
- **Audience Insights** shows the questions asked through assistants next to the ones asked in your portal.
