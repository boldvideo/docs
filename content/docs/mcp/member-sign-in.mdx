With **Members sign in**, nobody gets an answer without signing in on your site first. Your site decides who the member is and which collections they may use. Bold never sees a password and keeps no copy of your membership rules.

This needs a developer and a site of your own (a custom portal). It is one route on your server.

## How it works

1. A member adds your address in their assistant. The assistant sends their browser to Bold, and Bold sends it on to the **sign-in page on your site**: `https://yourdomain.com/connect?request=…`.
2. Your site makes sure the member is signed in, then tells Bold from your server who it is and which collections they may use.
3. Bold shows the member an **Allow** page with your name and logo. They choose **Allow** and return to the assistant.
4. About once an hour, while the member keeps asking, Bold asks your site again. Your answer replaces the old one: a member who bought another course gets it, a member who cancelled is disconnected.

## Set it up

### 1. In Bold

Under **Settings › Claude & ChatGPT › Who can use it**, choose **Members sign in** and enter the sign-in page on your site, for example `https://yourdomain.com/connect`. Your account needs the **Custom** portal type and a video link template under **Portal › Domain**, so that sources link to your site.

Then copy two secrets into your server's environment. Neither may ever reach a browser.

| Secret | Where | What it proves |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| **Server key** | **Settings › Developer**. Ask us for a key that may approve connections. | Your site to Bold |
| **Signing secret** | **Settings › Claude & ChatGPT** | Bold to your site |

### 2. On your site

With [`@boldvideo/bold-js`](/sdk) 3.1 or later, the whole route is one call. In a Next.js app, `app/connect/route.ts`:

```ts
import { createClient } from "@boldvideo/bold-js";

const bold = createClient(process.env.BOLD_CONNECT_API_KEY!);

export const { GET, POST } = bold.mcp.connect({
  signingSecret: process.env.BOLD_CONNECT_SIGNING_SECRET!,
  // Who is signed in? Return your own member ID, or nothing.
  viewer: async (req) => (await currentMember(req))?.id,
  // What may this member use? Return null for "nothing".
  access: async (memberId) => {
    const collections = await collectionsFor(memberId);
    return collections.length ? { answer: collections } : null;
  },
  // Where to send someone who is not signed in.
  signIn: "/login",
});
```

`currentMember` and `collectionsFor` are yours. `answer` is a list of Bold collection IDs.

The member must exist in Bold as a [viewer](/sdk/viewers) whose external ID is your member ID. Create or sync the viewer before you approve the connection.

### 3. Check it

Add your address in Claude with a test member. **Settings › Claude & ChatGPT** shows "Your site answered" once Bold's first check has passed.

## The two requests

If you don't use the SDK, these are the two requests to implement.

**Your site approves a connection.** After the member has signed in on `GET /connect?request=<id>`:

```http
PUT https://app.boldvideo.io/api/v1/mcp/connections/<id>
Authorization: Bearer <server key>
Content-Type: application/json

{ "viewer": "<your member ID>", "access": { "answer": ["<collection ID>"] } }
```

Bold answers `{ "continue_url": "https://…" }`. Redirect the browser there, after checking that it is HTTPS and on your MCP address.

**Bold asks again.** About once an hour per connected member:

```http
POST https://yourdomain.com/connect
Bold-Signature: t=<unix seconds>,v1=<hex HMAC-SHA256 of "<t>.<raw body>">
Content-Type: application/json

{ "viewer_id": "<Bold viewer ID>", "external_id": "<your member ID>" }
```

Verify the signature over the raw body with the signing secret, and reject a timestamp more than five minutes off. Then answer:

| Your answer | What Bold does |
|-------------|----------------|
| `200` with `{ "access": { "answer": [...] } }` | Uses the new list from now on |
| `404` | Ends this member's connection. They have to connect again. |
| Anything else | Keeps the last answer and asks again later |

<Callout type="warn" title="404 means gone for good">
  Answer `404` only when you know the member has lost access. If your membership system is down or slow, answer `500`. Otherwise an outage on your side disconnects paying members.
</Callout>

## Good to know

- **Turning it off:** use the switch in Bold. Removing the secrets from your site makes every hourly check fail with `404`, which disconnects everyone.
- **A new signing secret** takes effect at once. Update your site in the same step.
- **Switching back to "Anyone with the address"** ends every member's connection.
